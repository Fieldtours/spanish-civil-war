THE SPANISH CIVIL WAR 1936-1939 - A DZ CHARLIE FIELD GUIDE
Project started: 27 September 2026

WHAT IT IS
A free phone guide to 158 sites of the Spanish Civil War across the whole
of Spain: battlefields, trenches, ruined villages, air-raid shelters,
museums, cemeteries and memorials, in 8 areas. Visitors choose the area
they want to tour and how much time they have (2 hours to 2 weeks), and
the guide builds the route day by day with Google Maps directions.
Also: timeline (60 events), 6 step-by-step battle maps, 26 people from
both sides, 14 weapons, the coup hour by hour, words to know, a printable
PDF (73 pages), and it works with no signal. In English.

TECHNICAL NOTES (for Claude)
Single-file app (index.html) with its own offline map of Spain drawn from
Natural Earth data (no map tiles needed), own sw.js (cache scw-core-*),
localStorage keys prefixed scw-. Built from the Easy Company layout and
battle-map engine. Pictures: public domain / Creative Commons from
Wikimedia Commons, credited in the app (More > Credits).
