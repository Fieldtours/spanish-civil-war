## Open doubts
- **Coordinate verification had to work around the tools.** es.wikipedia, Wikidata, Nominatim and several Aragonese sites (huellasdelaguerraenaragon.com, sipca.es, alcubierre.es, turismodearagon.com, museodebielsa.com) could not be fetched, and the web-search budget ran out partway through. Towns taken from en.wikipedia coordinates are marked LOW or MEDIUM.
- **Teruel shelters (aragon-02):** the coordinates are the Wikivoyage city point, which is essentially Plaza del Torico. The exact entrance, opening hours and price were not verified.
- **Town-centre coordinates only:** Barbastro museum, Bielsa museum, Codo cross, Alcañiz shelter (the street address is known), Robres centre and Villarquemado centre.
- **Checked but not included:**
  - La Muela de Teruel: no verified coordinates.
  - Ermita de Salas: no Civil War source and no coordinates.
  - Mediana, Fraga, Calanda, Celadas and Alfambra as separate stops: no specific visitable site confirmed. Celadas and Alfambra are covered by the Villarquemado centre.
  - Gurrea de Gállego and Montes de Zuera: not researched after the search budget ran out.
  - Belchite seminary and Mojón del Lobo: they exist according to belchite.es, but no coordinates were found. They are mentioned only in the Belchite research and could be added.
  - The San Simón monument and Loma de los Cornudos: San Simón is mentioned in the Orwell stop; nothing was found on Loma de los Cornudos.
- **Facts not confirmed by a fetched source:**
  - Pilar bombs date (3 Aug 1936)
  - Fuentes de Ebro date (13 Oct 1937) and the Mac-Paps
  - Italian aircraft at Alcañiz
  - Bielsa pocket details (Beltrán, 16 Jun 1938)
  - Barbastro museum location and hours
- **Pozos de Caudé:** TracesOfWar says killings there were committed by both sides. Most accounts treat it as a Nationalist rearguard site. This is flagged in `disputed`.
- **Short texts:** stops 10, 16 and 20–24 are under 80 words. They are minor sites with little verified detail.
- **Who held Muela de Villaspesa:** the side is inferred, not confirmed.
