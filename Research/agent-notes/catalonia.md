## Open doubts
- **Móra d'Ebre castle (17)** and **La Vajol / Coll de Lli (26)** use town-centre coordinates (LOW). The castle, the exile monument and the Coll de Lli were not pinned down.
- **Vilalba Els Barrancs (12)**: the coordinates are for the Mirador del Bassot (MEDIUM). It is unclear from the sources which army built the Barrancs trenches.
- **Martyrs Chapel (22)**: TracesOfWar places it on Carrer del Bisbe (cathedral precinct). Its exact form and access are unverified (MEDIUM).
- **Serra de Pàndols**: the TracesOfWar memorial point is about 700 m from the Wikipedia summit (Punta Alta, 41.02047, 0.45774). I used the memorial point. The TracesOfWar inscription text (it mentions the "43rd Division") looks garbled and was not used.
- **Tortosa Francoist Ebro monument**: left out. I believe it was dismantled in 2024; please check.
- **Not included (no verifiable coordinates, or out of region):**
  - **Aeròdrom de Rosell:** Rosell is in Castellón province, so it belongs to the levant region.
  - **Batea "Hospitals de Sang" and Pinell de Brai "Veus del Front" centres:** not included.
  - **Ascó crossing / XV Army Corps camp:** only mentioned in the Flix and Camposines texts.
  - **Lleida refugi / Seu Vella:** not included.
  - **Talarn / Merengue trenches:** Mont de Conques + La Posa used for the Pallars front instead.
  - **Reus and Tarragona shelters:** not included.
  - **Plaça George Orwell:** mentioned in the Poliorama text only.
  - **Mas Perxés:** not included.
- Extra verified candidates not used:
  - **Serra del Tallat artillery observation post** (Vallbona de les Monges, 41.48258, 1.13840). I left it out because I couldn't confirm which army built it.
  - **Camarasa and Tossal de les Forques Nationalist trenches** (41.83538, 0.84302; 41.81559, 0.87379).
  - **Alfés airfield** (41.54976, 0.65227).
  - **L'Aranyó airfield** (41.72068, 1.21686).
  - **Refugi 232, Plaça del Diamant** (41.40389, 2.15583).
