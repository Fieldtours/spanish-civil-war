## Doubts / open items
- **Coordinates only at LOW certainty (town centre):** Otxandio (Andikona square), Zamudio trail start (Arroeta car park), Berango interpretation centre. **MEDIUM:** Elgeta centre (Espaloia, village centre), Sollube, San Marcial, Escudo, El Mazuco, Simancas memorial.
- **Not included (couldn't verify with coordinates or a source; the web-search budget ran out mid-task):** Monte Bizkargi, Kalamua, Lemoatx, Eibar, Peñas de Aia, Grado / El Escamplero bunkers, Oviedo Fábrica de armas de La Vega, Santoña.
- **Refugio de Cimavilla (Gijón):** left out on purpose because it is still being restored and isn't open yet (gijonturismoprofesional.es). Add it once it opens (Plaza de Revillagigedo access).
- **Francoist monuments:** the Oviedo "defenders" memorial (inside the town hall) and the Gijón Simancas memorial may have been removed or changed under the Democratic Memory Law. Their current status wasn't checked.
- **Balance:** there is no verified site yet for Republican-zone repression in the North (e.g. the Bilbao prison killings of 4 Jan 1937, or Santander's prison ship *Alfonso Pérez* / Pozo Tremeo). TracesOfWar lists a "Collective Grave Nationalists" at Derio cemetery (43.29223, -2.89029, sight 90363), but it has almost no information, so it wasn't used.
- TracesOfWar says Colegio El Carmelo in Amorebieta (90424) was a "Nationalist prison from 18 July 1936". That is doubtful, because Amorebieta was in the Republican zone until 1937, so it wasn't used.
- Intxorta's final withdrawal date (about 20–24 Apr 1937) is not confirmed by a primary source.
- Gernika death toll, Durango (248–336) and Otxandio (57–61 vs 84) are all marked as disputed.
- El Mazuco length: 5/6–22 Sep vs "33 days" (en.wikipedia Asturias Offensive).
