# South (Andalusia): research notes

16 stops in `south.json`, most important first.

## Coordinate verification
- **HIGH, checked against a source:** south-01 Almería shelters (Junta Agenda Cultural), south-02 Víznar grave 1 (desaparicionforzadadeandalucia.org), south-04 Huerta de San Vicente (en.wikipedia), south-05 Peñón del Cuervo / Paseo de los Canadienses (komoot highlight), south-06 Santuario (en.wikipedia).
- **MEDIUM, town-level source:** south-07 Lopera (the battle article's coordinates, which point at the town; the poets' garden and the machine-gun nests are not pinned), south-11 Villanueva de Córdoba (town centre; the shelter is under the Plaza de España, which is the centre).
- **Estimated from my own knowledge, not checked against a source. Re-check before release:**
  - south-03 Alfacar park (37.2399, -3.5563)
  - south-08 Torre del Mar memorial (near the bus station)
  - south-09 Málaga San Rafael pantheon
  - south-10 Seville San Fernando cemetery / Pico Reja
  - south-13 Puente de Maro
  - south-14 Almadrabillas
  - south-15 Carchuna fort
  - south-16 Tablada. The en.wikipedia "Tablada Air Base" URL redirected to the Seville Airport page, which is a different airfield (San Pablo). The coordinates are my estimate.
- The tools were limited: es.wikipedia, Wikidata and OSM/Nominatim were blocked or cache-only, and the web-search budget ran out partway through.

## Content taken from my own knowledge, not from a fetched source
- Huerta: guided visits only and closed Mondays.
- Pico Reja: excavations began in 2020. Queipo's Unión Radio broadcasts.
- Carchuna: the exact date of 23 May 1938. The source says only May 1938.
- Tablada/Jerez airlift details.
- Lorca: arrest on 16 Aug and the Rosales house. Fernández-Montesinos.
- Víznar: La Colonia.

## Dropped or not verified
- **Pozoblanco:** no visitable remains found. The battle is covered in the text for south-11.
- **Jaén and Úbeda shelters, Guadix, Castro del Río, Carmona, Algeciras:** nothing checked.
- **Seville Capitanía / Queipo radio site:** no exact site checked.
- **Blas Infante execution monument (km 4, Carretera de Carmona)** and the **Coria del Río museum:** no coordinates found. Both are mentioned in the text for south-10.

## Balance
- I found no dedicated memorial site in Andalusia for killings in the Republican zone.
  - Málaga's roughly 2,500 Republican-zone killings are noted in the text for south-09.
  - south-06 (Santuario) gives the Nationalist side.
