## Open doubts
- Valencia Town Hall shelter: the city's sources disagree on access. One says it is open Tuesday to Sunday for free, another says guided visits only, on Saturdays at 12:00 and 13:00.
- Viver "CIBAL" interpretation centre: known only from a 2021 blog post. Not confirmed that it exists or is open.
- Stanbrook plaque: its exact location in the port was not found, and it has been vandalised before. Passenger numbers vary: 2,638 vs "about 3,000".
- Castillitos "fired once in 1937": from a single secondary source. The Castillo de Olite was sunk by the La Parajola battery, not by Castillitos.
- Bayo landing end date: 4 September (es.wiki Porto Cristo) vs 12 September 1936 (en.wiki Battle of Majorca). There is no landing monument at Porto Cristo.
- Palau de Benicarló: the 1937 Writers' Congress and the May 1937 change of prime minister are standard history but were not re-checked against a fetched source. It is also not verified that the Palau (rather than another building) was the Presidencia for the whole period.
- Valencia bombing totals (more than 440 raids, about 800 dead) come only from Tourism Valencia.
- Left out because they could not be verified or have little to see on site: Sagunto, Dénia, Nules, Murcia city, Los Alcázares / El Carmolí airfields, Menorca, Vilafamés. Also left out: the Almenara–La Llosa bunkers (an official XYZ route exists but no coordinates were found) and the Alicante Plaza Balmis shelter (mentioned inside stop 07).
