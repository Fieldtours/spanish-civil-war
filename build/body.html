Briefing

How to use this guide

Plan

Open Tour, choose the part of Spain you will visit and how much time you have. The guide picks the most important sites that fit, puts them in driving order and splits them into days.

Go

Every stop has a Google Maps button for directions. Near me at the top shows the closest sites wherever you are.

Read

Each stop tells what happened there, what you can still see and how to plan the visit. Where historians disagree — about numbers killed, or who was responsible — a dashed box says so.

Accuracy

exact spot known · right area · general area only. Tap any rating for more.

How it began

From the birth of the Second Republic in April 1931 to the military rising of July 1936.

People

Leaders, commanders, volunteers and witnesses — from both sides and from abroad.

Battle maps

Six battles drawn as simple step-by-step sketches: who was where, who attacked, and how it ended. Red boxes are Republican units, blue diamonds Nationalist units — the usual colours of historical maps of this war.

Weapons & arms

Spain's own arsenals, Soviet aid to the Republic, and German and Italian aid to the Nationalists: the war was also a testing ground for the weapons of the Second World War.

Official museums, visitor centres and routes. Check opening times before you go: many small centres open only at weekends or by appointment.
